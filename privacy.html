Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

TagLoom is an early-stage tool that helps Etsy sellers research keywords and improve listing tags. TagLoom is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy, Inc.

What we collect

Right now, only the email address you enter on the waitlist form. We use it to tell you when early access opens. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

Etsy data

When the tool launches, it will read publicly available Etsy listing information (such as titles, tags, and prices) through Etsy's official API, and may cache it temporarily to stay within rate limits. If you choose to connect your own shop, we will request read-only access to your listings and nothing else. We will not access orders, customer information, or payment details. You can disconnect at any time.

Storage and security

Data is stored with reputable hosting providers. API credentials are kept on the server and never exposed in your browser.
