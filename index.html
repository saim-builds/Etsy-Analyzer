Three tools for one job: getting your listing found

Keyword research

Type a phrase and see demand, competition, and an opportunity score. Related long-tail phrases come sorted by how easy they are to win.

Listing audit

Paste a listing link. We flag unused tags, near-duplicates, title length, and keywords that most top listings use but yours doesn't.

Tag suggestions

Get a ready set of 13 tags, each under 20 characters, matched to your title. Copy them straight into Etsy.

Opportunity is demand weighed against competition

Etsy doesn't publish real search volume, so we estimate demand from autocomplete suggestions, trend data, and how often buyers favorite top listings.

Competition comes from the number of results and how many reviews the leading listings have. A high opportunity score means buyers are looking and the field isn't crowded.
